Client Dashboard

About The Victorias Ltd Client Dashboard

A complete guide to every feature in your dashboard, what data we collect to power it, how long we keep it, and how to stay in control of your account at all times.

Overview

What is the Dashboard?

The Victorias Client Dashboard is your private portal for managing your relationship with our AI-assisted business services. It gives you real-time visibility into your deliverables, quotas, communications, and connected accounts — all in one place, secured behind a Client ID and access code.

Overview & Quotas

A single glance at your Ops and/or Digital plan: documents, emails, mailboxes, WhatsApp, and social quotas — shown against your monthly allotment (or PAYG usage). AI work that delivers those jobs is included; it is never billed by the hour. Human time is a separate add-on. Social cards show published posts and engagement when results are recorded.

Generate & Track Documents

Self-generate any of 100+ document types — proposals, reports, receipts, agreements, and more — for one recipient or many at once. Multiple documents are developed in safe batches. Use Preview layout on a card for a free sample (does not count against your allocation). Browse everything delivered under My Documents; filter by title, type, source, and date.

My Work

Live status for anything currently being generated on your behalf, with the option to stop a job in progress if you no longer need it.

Live Chat with Your Team

Direct messaging with your account team for paid admin work. Chat unlocks when you have dedicated human time left (bought under Billing). When that balance reaches zero, admin chat locks until you add more human time. Support tickets and bug reports stay available without human-time balance.

Social Calendar & Approvals

Review each month's planned posts by batch, and approve or reject them individually — or all at once — before they go live. Overview reflects published counts from this calendar; engagement fills in once performance is recorded.

Appointments

Every meeting or call scheduled on your behalf, filterable by status — upcoming, planned, past, or cancelled — and by date range.

Email Activity Log

See every message the agent has handled in your connected mailbox — what it classified, ignored, drafted, or sent — with the reply text where applicable. Newest first, paginated 50 at a time.

WhatsApp Activity

Inbound messages on your Cloud API number(s), agent replies, and referrals to linked staff WhatsApp numbers. Filter by direct (API) or indirect (team). Revoke any connection anytime.

Integrations Management

View and revoke connected mailboxes, WhatsApp numbers, CRM connections, and social accounts — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, and Threads (higher Digital tiers) — at any time, directly from the dashboard.

Support Us (Donations)

Optional one-time donations via secure Flutterwave checkout. You choose the amount; card details are handled by Flutterwave — we never see them.

Compliance & Privacy Controls

Toggle mailbox privacy so our admin team cannot see messages the agent has replied to in that mailbox, and control whether receipts and confirmations auto-send or wait for your approval.

In Detail

How Each Tab Works

A closer look at the tabs in your dashboard sidebar, so you always know what a button or toggle actually does before you use it.

Generate a Document

Each document type is shown as a card that's either active or greyed out, based on your own plan's remaining document allocation. Active cards expand into an instruction panel — fill in the requested fields and click Send. Preview layout builds a free sample with placeholder values so you can check structure before generating; it does not use document units. You can optionally list multiple recipients (one per line); shared fields apply to everyone. Larger documents (reports, plans, proposals) are generated and filed straight into My Documents. Lightweight ones (receipts, payment confirmations) either email out immediately or wait in "Pending Your Approval," depending on your Auto-Send preference above the cards.

Multiple recipients & batches

When you generate the same document for several people at once, the system develops them in batches so processing stays reliable. Batch size is always under 10 and depends on how heavy the document type is (lighter financial docs allow larger batches; heavy proposals or legal docs run fewer at a time). The dashboard tells you how many batches were used when the job finishes.

Document weight & templates

Each document type has a weight from 1 (e.g. receipt) to 5 (heavy legal or compliance). Your allocation is charged by weight × number of documents successfully generated. When your account team confirms a sample or blank template for a type, future runs follow that pattern unless a new pattern is approved — confirming a template is charged as one document of equivalent weight for that type.

Background Generation

Longer documents are queued and generated in the background — you're free to navigate away, and the dashboard polls automatically for a result. You'll also get an in-app notification and, if it's taking a while, an email the moment it's ready.

Approve & Send

Documents tagged "Pending Your Approval" in My Documents (receipts, confirmations, etc.) stay put until you explicitly click Approve & Send — this uses one email credit from your plan and is entirely your call.

Social Calendar

Your account team plans and drafts each month's posts; reviewing them here is optional. Posts still go live once your account team approves them either way — approving or rejecting on your end simply registers your own say-so alongside theirs, with an optional note if you reject one.

Post Performance

A "Performance" panel on the Social Calendar tab (and the Social publishing card on Overview) shows how your published posts have actually done — impressions, likes, and engagement rate per post, plus platform-level averages. Published counts come from the live calendar; Posts tracked and engagement stay at zero until results are recorded for those posts. This is the same data your account team's agent studies to sharpen future posts for you (and, in aggregate, for other clients too).

Email Activity

Messages the agent has handled in your connected mailbox appear here with classification status and any drafted or sent reply. Newest first. Your account team can enable auto-send for routine replies, or hold drafts for approval.

WhatsApp Activity

API inbox messages, replies, and team referrals. Filter by API number or staff link. Processing may count toward plan hours and message usage.

Mailbox Privacy

Each connected mailbox has its own visibility switch. Set a mailbox to Private and our admin team can no longer see the message content the agent has replied to in that box — you retain full visibility either way.

Integrations

Mailboxes, WhatsApp numbers, CRM connections, and social accounts — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and on Digital Scale also X and Threads — are listed with their live status. Revoking a connection stops the agent from using it immediately; reconnecting is a click away or via your account team.

Support Us

Optional one-time donations through Flutterwave. You enter an amount and are redirected to secure checkout; we never receive or store your card details.

Notifications

The bell icon in the header surfaces real-time alerts — a generated document finishing or failing — and links straight to the relevant tab. Mark individual items or everything as read at any time.

Quotas, Pricing & Discount Codes

Quotas (documents, emails, proposals, social posts, etc.) match your Ops and Digital tiers. Open Billing for the only full price list, upgrades, and add-ons. Discount codes may still apply when your account team issues them.

My Work

A live, auto-refreshing list of jobs currently queued or running for your account, with the option to terminate one in progress. Note that a terminated job still counts against your document quota for the period.

Privacy & Data

What Data We Collect & Why

We collect only what is necessary to deliver our services effectively. Your data is always handled in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and our own strict internal policies.

Account & Identity
  • Client ID and access code — used to authenticate and secure your session.
  • Company name and contact details — so we know who we're serving and can send important notifications.
  • Subscription tiers (Ops / Digital), add-ons, billing region, usage metrics, and human-time balance — to manage plan and quotas accurately.
Documents & Content
  • Self-generated documents — anything you create via the Generate a Document tab, including the instructions you provide.
  • Admin-delivered documents — reports, proposals, and compliance files sent by our team.
  • Document metadata — title, type, creation date, source, and approval status — to organise your history.
  • Uploaded samples and confirmed document templates — so generation can follow your house style.
Email & Communication
  • Incoming email messages (sender, subject, body summary) — so the agent can draft context-aware replies.
  • AI-generated replies — stored so you can see exactly what was sent and to help us improve drafting quality.
  • Mailbox labels, connection status, and your per-mailbox privacy setting — to manage which boxes the agent monitors and who can see their content.
Integrations
  • CRM connections (HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce) — we pull read-only data to provide pipeline insights and reporting.
  • Social media accounts — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn on every plan; X and Threads on Digital Scale — accessed only to publish pre-approved posts on your behalf, within your plan's per-platform quota (and video, on Digital Scale).
  • WhatsApp Cloud API numbers (read/reply) and optional staff WhatsApp links for human referrals — within plan limits.
Scheduling & Social
  • Appointment details — title, attendees, date/time, and type — for meetings scheduled on your behalf.
  • Social calendar batches — captions, hashtags, visual suggestions, and your approval or rejection decision on each post.
  • Post performance metrics — impressions, likes, and engagement rate on your published posts — used to improve future recommendations for you, and in aggregate, across our client base.
Chat & Notifications
  • Live Chat messages with your account team (when human-time balance allows), plus support tickets and bug reports.
  • In-app notifications — e.g. a generated document completing or failing — and whether you've read them.

We never sell your data to third parties. All data is stored securely in Google Workspace and encrypted where required.

Retention

How Long Do We Keep Your Data?

We retain data only as long as necessary to serve you or to comply with legal obligations. Below are our typical retention periods.

Data type Retention period
Account information Duration of your active subscription, plus a reasonable administrative period after termination.
Documents & deliverables Kept in your client folder indefinitely unless you request deletion.
Live Chat messages 3 days, then automatically pruned as a housekeeping measure.
Audit logs 5 days, then automatically deleted.
OAuth tokens & API credentials Stored encrypted in our integration registry; revocable at any time from the Integrations tab.
Email content We keep a summary of processed messages; full raw content is not permanently stored. Deletion of logs available on request.
Social media posts Kept until published; post metadata retained afterward for audit purposes.
Appointments & notifications Kept for the duration of your active subscription to preserve your activity history.

You may request complete data export or deletion at any time via the dashboard or by emailing management@thevictorias.com.ng.

Plans

Ops Suite + Digital Suite

Your account can include an Ops plan, a Digital plan, or both at the same time. When both are active, the dashboard sidebar and Billing tab show both names — one is never hidden behind the other. Quotas and features from each suite apply side by side.

Ops Suite

Documents, proposals, emails, mailboxes, WhatsApp, and related ops quotas. AI that powers those deliverables is included. Dedicated human time is purchased as an add-on when you need the account team to do hands-on work.

Digital Suite

Social calendar, content assets, multi-platform publishing (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn; more platforms on Digital Push), and related digital quotas. Video posts and paid amplification are add-ons unless your Digital tier includes a small allowance.

Billing

How Charges Work

All prices, upgrades, and add-ons appear in one place only: the Billing tab in your dashboard. Nigeria (local NGN) and offshore (USD) lists both use the same structure. Exchange rate is set by us and shown on Billing. AI is included in the jobs you run — we do not sell AI time by the hour.

Ops & Digital subscriptions

Ops (Lite / Grow / Firm) covers documents, email, mailboxes, and WhatsApp quotas. Digital (Start / Steady / Push) covers social posts, platforms, calendar, and reports. You can hold one suite or both. Self-upgrade from Billing when you need higher limits.

Local example (NGN/mo): Ops from about ₦29,000 · Digital from about ₦24,000. Offshore list is in USD (e.g. Ops from $49).

Add-ons (not inside base tiers)

Sold only as add-ons: dedicated human time, CRM connection, lead generation, compliance document tracking, custom document training, social asset packs, extra video slots, and paid amplification seat (ad spend stays yours). Human time packs unlock admin chat for requesting that work.

Pay-as-you-go

Wallet top-ups with fixed unit prices for light/heavy documents, email batches, and social publishes. No separate “AI hour” line — the job price covers AI delivery. Exact rates are listed on your Billing tab.

Support & system load

Open a support ticket or report a bug from Support — no human-time balance required. If the platform is under heavy load you may see a short cooldown instead of a crash; retry after the countdown.

Live payment on self-upgrade may be confirmed by your account team until online checkout is fully linked; entitlements still follow the same catalog.

Control

How to Revoke Access

You are in control. You can disconnect any integrated service, adjust mailbox privacy, or manage auto-send preferences directly from the dashboard without needing to contact us.

Integrations Tab
  • Go to the Integrations section of your dashboard.
  • Under Mailboxes, WhatsApp, CRM, or Social Media (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Threads), click Revoke next to any connection.
  • The agent immediately stops reading from or sending through that service — reconnect any time with one click or via your account team.

Revoking does not delete past data; it only prevents future access.

Contact Support
  • To delete your entire account or data, email management@thevictorias.com.ng.
  • We process your request within 48 hours and confirm once it's done.
  • You can also end your own session any time by clicking Sign Out in the dashboard.

Rights

Your Privacy Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act and other applicable regulations, you have the following rights regarding your data.

Right to Access

You can view all data we hold about you in the dashboard. You may also request a full export in a machine-readable format.

Right to Rectification

If any of your account details are incorrect, you can update them via your dashboard or by contacting your account manager.

Right to Erasure

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. We will comply unless we have a legal obligation to retain certain records.
