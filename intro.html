About The Victorias Ltd WebApp

Everything you need to know about how our dashboard works, what data we collect, and how your information is handled.

Overview

What is the WebApp?

The Victorias Dashboard is your private portal in the WebApp to manage your relationship with our AI‑assisted business services. It gives you real‑time visibility into your deliverables, compliance status, and communications—all in one place.

Generate & Track Documents

Request proposals, reports, pitch decks, and compliance documents on demand. See their status and download them when ready.

Live Chat with Your Team

Direct messaging with your account team—all conversations are saved so nothing is lost if you refresh or close the tab.

Social Calendar & Approvals

Review, approve, or reject proposed social posts before they go live. Optionally add your feedback.

Email Activity Log

See every email the agent has replied to on your behalf—along with the actual reply sent—so you always stay in the loop.

Integrations Management

View and revoke connected mailboxes, social media accounts, and CRM connections at any time—directly from the dashboard.

Compliance & Privacy Controls

Toggle mailbox privacy to hide message details from our admin team, and see your compliance posture at a glance.

Privacy & Data

What Data We Collect & Why

We collect only what is necessary to deliver our services effectively. Your data is always handled in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and our own strict internal policies.

Account & Identity
  • Client ID and access code – used to authenticate and secure your session.
  • Company name and contact details – so we know who we're serving and can send important notifications.
  • Subscription tier and usage metrics – to manage your plan and quota.
Documents & Content
  • Self‑generated documents – what you create via the Generate a Document tab.
  • Admin‑delivered documents – reports, proposals, and compliance files sent by our team.
  • Document metadata – title, type, creation date, and status – to organise your history.
Email & Communication
  • Incoming email messages (sender, subject, body snippet) – so the agent can draft context‑aware replies.
  • AI‑generated replies – we store these to show you what was sent and to improve our drafting.
  • Mailbox labels and connection status – to manage which boxes the agent monitors.
Integrations
  • CRM connections (HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce) – we pull read‑only data to provide pipeline insights and reporting.
  • Social media accounts – we access only to publish pre‑approved posts on your behalf.
  • WhatsApp Business numbers – to read and reply to incoming messages.

We never sell your data to third parties. All data is stored securely in Google Workspace and encrypted where required.

Retention

How Long Do We Keep Your Data?

We retain data only as long as necessary to serve you or to comply with legal obligations. Below are the typical retention periods.

Regular Data
  • Account information – kept for the duration of your active subscription, plus a reasonable period after termination for administrative purposes.
  • Documents & deliverables – stored in your client folder indefinitely unless you request deletion.
  • Chat messages – kept for 3 days after which they are automatically pruned. (This is a safety measure to keep the chat sheet manageable.)
  • Audit logs – retained for 5 days then automatically deleted.
Sensitive Data
  • OAuth tokens & API credentials – stored encrypted in our integration registry. You can revoke them at any time (see below).
  • Email content – we keep a summary of processed messages; full raw content is not permanently stored. You may request deletion of your email logs by contacting support.
  • Social media posts – kept until published; after publishing we retain the post metadata for audit purposes.

You may request complete data export or deletion at any time via the dashboard or by emailing management@thevictorias.com.ng.

Control

How to Revoke Access

You are in control. You can disconnect any integrated service directly from the dashboard without needing to contact us.

Integrations Tab
  • Go to the Integrations section of your dashboard.
  • Under Mailboxes, WhatsApp, Social Media, or CRM, click the Revoke button next to any connection.
  • The agent will immediately stop reading from or sending through that service.

Revoking does not delete past data; it only prevents future access.

Contact Support
  • If you want to delete your entire account or data, email management@thevictorias.com.ng.
  • We will process your request within 48 hours and confirm once it's done.
  • You can also deactivate your own session by clicking Sign Out in the dashboard.

Rights

Your Privacy Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act and other applicable regulations, you have the following rights regarding your data.

Right to Access

You can view all data we hold about you in the dashboard. You may also request a full export in a machine‑readable format.

Right to Rectification

If any of your account details are incorrect, you can update them via your dashboard or by contacting your account manager.

Right to Erasure

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. We will comply unless we have a legal obligation to retain certain records.

For any privacy‑related questions, please email management@thevictorias.com.ng.
