About The Victorias Ltd Client Dashboard

Your private portal for AI-assisted ops, documents, messaging, and growth workflows. Built in Abuja, Nigeria — designed for companies with local and international operations.

Overview

What is the Dashboard?

Real-time visibility into deliverables, quotas, communications, and connected accounts — secured with a Client ID and access code. Subscription or Pay-as-you-go billing is managed under Billing & Renewal.

Overview & Quotas

Hours, emails, proposals, documents against plan limits — or wallet balance if you are on Pay-as-you-go.

Generate & Track Documents

Self-generate by industry and category (Universal, Schools, NGO, and more), or browse team deliverables.

Billing: Subscription or PAYG

Renew a plan term with optional discount codes, or top up a Pay-as-you-go wallet. Unit rates cover ops (hours, emails, documents) and digital (posts ≤ ₦8,000, calendar batches ≤ ₦8,000).

Live Chat

Message your account team; history is saved to your account.

Social Calendar

Review planned posts, approve or reject, and view performance insights.

WhatsApp Activity

Cloud API inbox, agent replies, and referrals to linked staff numbers.

Email Activity

Handled messages, drafts, and sent replies with mailbox privacy controls.

Integrations

Mailboxes, WhatsApp, CRM, and social accounts — revoke or reconnect anytime.

Privacy Controls

Mailbox privacy toggles and auto-send preferences for receipts and confirmations.

Billing

Subscription vs Pay-as-you-go

Your account is either on a monthly subscription tier or a prepaid wallet. Both are visible under Billing & Renewal in the dashboard.

Subscription

Fixed monthly quotas (hours, emails, proposals, documents, social). Renew for 1–12 months; apply discount codes from your account team when offered.

Pay-as-you-go

Top up your wallet in the dashboard (from ₦10,000). Usage debits published unit rates for ops and digital work. Social posts and calendar draft batches are priced at or below ₦8,000 per unit.

Privacy & Data

What Data We Collect & Why

Only what is needed to deliver the service. Handled under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and our internal policies. We do not sell your data.

Account & identity
  • Client ID, access code, company name, contacts
  • Plan tier or PAYG balance and usage metrics
Documents & channels
  • Generated and delivered documents and field inputs
  • Email and WhatsApp activity needed for agent replies
  • Social calendar captions and publish metadata

Control
