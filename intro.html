Client Dashboard

About The Victorias Ltd Client Dashboard

A complete guide to every feature in your dashboard, what data we collect to power it, how long we keep it, and how to stay in control of your account at all times.

Overview

What is the Dashboard?

The Victorias Client Dashboard is your private portal for managing your relationship with our AI-assisted business services. It gives you real-time visibility into your deliverables, quotas, communications, and connected accounts — all in one place, secured behind a Client ID and access code.

Overview & Quotas

A single glance at your plan: hours used, emails sent, proposals drafted, and documents delivered — each shown against your plan's monthly allotment, plus your most recent documents.

Generate & Track Documents

Self-generate any of 12 document types — proposals, reports, action plans, agreements, and more — or browse everything your account team has delivered. Filter by title, type, source, and date.

My Work

Live status for anything currently being generated on your behalf, with the option to stop a job in progress if you no longer need it.

Live Chat with Your Team

Direct messaging with your account team — every conversation is saved to your account so nothing is lost if you refresh or close the tab.

Social Calendar & Approvals

Review each month's planned posts by batch, and approve or reject them individually — or all at once — before they go live.

Appointments

Every meeting or call scheduled on your behalf, filterable by status — upcoming, planned, past, or cancelled — and by date range.

Email Activity Log

See every message the agent has seen in your connected mailbox, what it decided, and exactly what it replied with — paginated, 50 at a time.

Integrations Management

View and revoke connected mailboxes, WhatsApp numbers, CRM connections, and social accounts — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and (Digital Scale only) X and Threads — at any time, directly from the dashboard.

Compliance & Privacy Controls

Toggle mailbox privacy so our admin team cannot see messages the agent has replied to in that mailbox, and control whether receipts and confirmations auto-send or wait for your approval.

In Detail

How Each Tab Works

A closer look at the nine tabs in your dashboard sidebar, so you always know what a button or toggle actually does before you use it.

Generate a Document

Each document type is shown as a card that's either active or greyed out, based on your own plan's remaining quota for that period. Active cards expand into an instruction panel — fill in the requested fields and click Send. Larger documents (reports, plans, proposals) are generated and filed straight into My Documents. Lightweight ones (receipts, payment confirmations) either email out immediately or wait in "Pending Your Approval," depending on your Auto-Send preference above the cards.

Background Generation

Longer documents are queued and generated in the background — you're free to navigate away, and the dashboard polls automatically for a result. You'll also get an in-app notification and, if it's taking a while, an email the moment it's ready.

Approve & Send

Documents tagged "Pending Your Approval" in My Documents (receipts, confirmations, etc.) stay put until you explicitly click Approve & Send — this uses one email credit from your plan and is entirely your call.

Social Calendar

Your account team plans and drafts each month's posts; reviewing them here is optional. Posts still go live once your account team approves them either way — approving or rejecting on your end simply registers your own say-so alongside theirs, with an optional note if you reject one.

Post Performance

A "Performance" panel on the Social Calendar tab shows how your published posts have actually done — impressions, likes, and engagement rate per post, plus platform-level averages. This is the same data your account team's agent studies to sharpen future posts for you (and, in aggregate, for other clients too).

Mailbox Privacy

Each connected mailbox has its own visibility switch. Set a mailbox to Private and our admin team can no longer see the message content the agent has replied to in that box — you retain full visibility either way.

Integrations

Mailboxes, WhatsApp numbers, CRM connections, and social accounts — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and on Digital Scale also X and Threads — are listed with their live status. Revoking a connection stops the agent from using it immediately; reconnecting is a click away or via your account team.

Notifications

The bell icon in the header surfaces real-time alerts — a generated document finishing or failing — and links straight to the relevant tab. Mark individual items or everything as read at any time.

Quotas & Pricing

Every quota shown (hours, emails, proposals, documents, and self-service documents) reflects your plan's real, current limit — including any custom pricing your account manager has applied — not an estimate.

My Work

A live, auto-refreshing list of jobs currently queued or running for your account, with the option to terminate one in progress. Note that a terminated job still counts against your document quota for the period.

Privacy & Data

What Data We Collect & Why

We collect only what is necessary to deliver our services effectively. Your data is always handled in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and our own strict internal policies.

Account & Identity
  • Client ID and access code — used to authenticate and secure your session.
  • Company name and contact details — so we know who we're serving and can send important notifications.
  • Subscription tier, plan pricing, and usage metrics — to manage your plan and quota accurately.
Documents & Content
  • Self-generated documents — anything you create via the Generate a Document tab, including the instructions you provide.
  • Admin-delivered documents — reports, proposals, and compliance files sent by our team.
  • Document metadata — title, type, creation date, source, and approval status — to organise your history.
Email & Communication
  • Incoming email messages (sender, subject, body summary) — so the agent can draft context-aware replies.
  • AI-generated replies — stored so you can see exactly what was sent and to help us improve drafting quality.
  • Mailbox labels, connection status, and your per-mailbox privacy setting — to manage which boxes the agent monitors and who can see their content.
Integrations
  • CRM connections (HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce) — we pull read-only data to provide pipeline insights and reporting.
  • Social media accounts — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn on every plan; X and Threads on Digital Scale — accessed only to publish pre-approved posts on your behalf, within your plan's per-platform quota (and video, on Digital Scale).
  • WhatsApp Business numbers — to read and reply to incoming messages, within your plan's weekly reply quota where one applies.
Scheduling & Social
  • Appointment details — title, attendees, date/time, and type — for meetings scheduled on your behalf.
  • Social calendar batches — captions, hashtags, visual suggestions, and your approval or rejection decision on each post.
  • Post performance metrics — impressions, likes, and engagement rate on your published posts — used to improve future recommendations for you, and in aggregate, across our client base.
Chat & Notifications
  • Live Chat messages between you and your account team.
  • In-app notifications — e.g. a generated document completing or failing — and whether you've read them.

We never sell your data to third parties. All data is stored securely in Google Workspace and encrypted where required.

Retention

How Long Do We Keep Your Data?

We retain data only as long as necessary to serve you or to comply with legal obligations. Below are our typical retention periods.

Data type Retention period
Account information Duration of your active subscription, plus a reasonable administrative period after termination.
Documents & deliverables Kept in your client folder indefinitely unless you request deletion.
Live Chat messages 3 days, then automatically pruned as a housekeeping measure.
Audit logs 5 days, then automatically deleted.
OAuth tokens & API credentials Stored encrypted in our integration registry; revocable at any time from the Integrations tab.
Email content We keep a summary of processed messages; full raw content is not permanently stored. Deletion of logs available on request.
Social media posts Kept until published; post metadata retained afterward for audit purposes.
Appointments & notifications Kept for the duration of your active subscription to preserve your activity history.

You may request complete data export or deletion at any time via the dashboard or by emailing management@thevictorias.com.ng.

Control

How to Revoke Access

You are in control. You can disconnect any integrated service, adjust mailbox privacy, or manage auto-send preferences directly from the dashboard without needing to contact us.

Integrations Tab
  • Go to the Integrations section of your dashboard.
  • Under Mailboxes, WhatsApp, CRM, or Social Media (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Threads), click Revoke next to any connection.
  • The agent immediately stops reading from or sending through that service — reconnect any time with one click or via your account team.

Revoking does not delete past data; it only prevents future access.

Contact Support
  • To delete your entire account or data, email management@thevictorias.com.ng.
  • We process your request within 48 hours and confirm once it's done.
  • You can also end your own session any time by clicking Sign Out in the dashboard.

Rights

Your Privacy Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act and other applicable regulations, you have the following rights regarding your data.

Right to Access

You can view all data we hold about you in the dashboard. You may also request a full export in a machine-readable format.

Right to Rectification

If any of your account details are incorrect, you can update them via your dashboard or by contacting your account manager.

Right to Erasure

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. We will comply unless we have a legal obligation to retain certain records.

For any privacy-related questions, please email management@thevictorias.com.ng.
