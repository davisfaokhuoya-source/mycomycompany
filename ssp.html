🚀 Victorias Career Hub Training Academy

Your guided journey from Launch Specialist to Executive Partner. Complete each level with discipline and integrity.

Focus: Helping individuals move from “idea” to “entity”

Key Services: Company Registration (Inc.), Business Name, Graphics Design, CV Development

Training Insight

Level 1 is about Speed and Professionalism. Your goal is volume and first impressions. Teach clients that registering a Business Name is good, but upgrading to a Limited Liability Company gives them access to corporate bank accounts and investor trust from day one.

Recommended Pitch

"Don’t just get a Business Name. Upgrade to a Company (Limited Liability) for better banking access and investor confidence right from the start."

Success Goal

High volume of quality submissions. After 3 successful Company Registrations, you unlock Level 2 and receive a “Level 1 Digital Badge” via email.

Focus: Protecting and scaling already-registered businesses

Key Services: Annual Filings, SCUML Registration, Social Media Management, Basic Web Development

Training Insight

Shift from launching to avoiding pain. Many businesses get registered but later face CAC penalties for non-filing. Your role is to keep them compliant and growing.

Recommended Pitch

"Your business is registered, but is it compliant? Avoid daily CAC fines with timely Annual Filings and protect your operations with SCUML."

Success Goal

Consistent quality submissions and client retention. Strong performers move to Level 3.

Focus: Building and safeguarding brand assets

Key Services: Website Development, Trademarks, Advanced Company Profiles, Professional Proposals

Training Insight

Help clients secure their future. A strong brand and online presence protect them from copycats and open doors to bigger opportunities.

Recommended Pitch

"What happens if someone else registers your logo tomorrow? Secure your brand with a Trademark and build a professional website that attracts real clients."

Success Goal

At this level you can request your official Training Certificate from the back office. Maintain high standards to prepare for Level 4.

Focus: High-stakes strategic support for large organizations

Key Services: Procurement Support, Contract Management, Document Submission & Follow-up, Bidding Support, Compliance for Government/Corporate Contracts

Training Insight

This level requires a shift from “selling” to Strategic Consulting. You will handle sensitive corporate documents and help companies win or maintain multi-million Naira contracts.

Document Submission & Follow-up is critical here — accuracy and timeliness can make or break a client’s bid.

Recommended Pitch

"Do you have all the required certificates (PenCom, ITF, NSITF, etc.) ready for that government or corporate contract? Let us handle the paperwork and follow-up so you can focus on winning the deal."

Unlock Requirement

Level 4 is reserved for exceptional partners. You must complete 100 successful verified tasks and may need to pass a short “Procurement Basics” quiz.

Ready to Level Up?

You are eligible to request your official Training Certificate.
