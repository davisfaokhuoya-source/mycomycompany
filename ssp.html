🚀 Victorias Career Hub Training Academy

Your guided journey from Launch Specialist to Executive Partner. Master lead generation, value creation, and professional excellence. Calculate service cost with value calculator link - www.thevictorias.com.ng/value

Focus: Helping individuals move from “idea” to “entity”

Key Services: Company Registration (Inc.), Business Name, Graphics, CV Development

Training Insight

Level 1 is about **Speed and Professionalism**. Your role is to help people turn ideas into legal entities quickly and correctly. Always emphasize upgrading from a simple Business Name to a Limited Liability Company — it opens doors to corporate bank accounts and investor trust from day one.

Recommended Pitch

"Don't just get a Business Name; upgrade to a Company (Limited Liability) to get better access to corporate bank accounts and investor trust right from day one."

General Tips for Success at This Level
  • Focus on volume while maintaining high professionalism
  • Always respond to leads within 24 hours
  • Provide clear, honest timelines to build trust
  • Treat every client as your first step into the Victorias ecosystem
Daily Lead Generation Strategies

Proven Channels (Aim for 2–3 every day):

  1. Social Media Groups – Join active business communities on Facebook, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn. Always give value before asking.
  2. Personal Networks – Church, mosque, alumni, NYSC, family, friends, former colleagues.
  3. Content Creation – Post short educational videos or posts: “5 Reasons Your Business Needs CAC Registration”
  4. Cold Outreach – Polite, professional messages to business owners.
  5. Referrals – Ask satisfied clients to introduce you to others.

Daily Target for Level 1: Speak to at least 10 new prospects every single day.

How to Pitch Professionally & Close Deals
Professional 60-Second Pitch:
“Hello [Name], my name is [Your Name] with The Victorias Career Hub. We specialize in helping business owners register their companies with CAC and handle all necessary compliance documents quickly and correctly. Many of our clients tell us we save them weeks of stress and unnecessary costs. Would you be open to a quick discussion about your business registration needs?”

Key Closing Techniques:
  • Ask open questions about their timeline and challenges
  • Emphasize speed, reliability, and our track record
  • Offer to send the simple process and pricing
Work Ethics, Integrity & Personal Branding
Non-Negotiable Rules:
  • Always be honest — never exaggerate timelines or commissions
  • Respond to clients and the back office within 24 hours
  • Keep all client information confidential
  • Submit only accurate and complete client data
  • Represent The Victorias name with pride and professionalism at all times

Your personal brand matters. Every interaction you have is a reflection of this Hub. High ethics = faster approvals, higher retention, and quicker level advancement.

How to Submit Clients Correctly in the Portal
  1. Log into your dashboard and go to “Submit Client”
  2. Select only active tasks shown in the dropdown
  3. Fill every field accurately (especially phone and email)
  4. Attach supporting documents when available
  5. Double-check before submitting

Important: Submitting fake or incomplete information can lead to account suspension. Accuracy builds trust and faster payouts.

Success Goal

Aim for consistent high-volume quality submissions. After 3 successful Company Registrations, you will be promoted to Level 2 and receive a "Level 1 Digital Badge".

  • Always double-check submissions for accuracy
  • Emphasize how your service saves them time and money
Success Goal
