    When the agent refers a chat to your team

    The agent answers routine chats on your API WhatsApp number. On referral it sends a WhatsApp message from that API number to a linked staff number (by department) with the customer’s details:

    • Serious buyer / high intent — ready to purchase, quote with budget, or paid engagement.
    • Appointment / demo request — call, meeting, or visit needing a human.
    • Complaint or sensitive matter — billing disputes, legal, safety, reputation.
    • Explicit human request — “speak to someone”, “transfer to sales/support”.
    • Agent uncertainty — outside policy or only your staff can decide.

    Tag each linked number with a department. The agent picks a matching staff WhatsApp; if none match, any active linked number.

    Auto-send receipts & confirmations

    On: email the recipient as soon as the document is generated. Off: generate only and hold in My Documents as "Pending Your Approval" until you click Approve & Send.

    Document units (capacity)

    Each document uses 1–5 units of your plan capacity, based on complexity.

    • Weight 1 — Receipts, invoices, order confirmations · 1 unit · up to 9 per batch
    • Weight 2 — Simple letters & notices · 2 units · up to 6 per batch
    • Weight 3 — Standard reports & forms · 3 units · up to 4 per batch
    • Weight 4 — Proposals & pitch decks · 4 units · up to 2 per batch · 1 proposal ≈ 4 receipts
    • Weight 5 — Legal / compliance · 5 units · 1 per batch

    Multi-client runs

    The open form is client #1. + Add client opens a full form for each extra person. Units = weight × number of clients. Large runs split into batches automatically; one Send still starts the whole job.

    Trained templates & line items

    If a sample was trained for your account, layout and fields follow your design. Itemized docs always allow + Add line for multiple products or services.

    Tips

    • Preview layout checks design before spending units on a full send.
    • Import from form #1 copies the main form into an extra client (only when you have 2+ clients).
    • Finished files appear under My Documents; long jobs may also email you when ready.
    • If capacity is short, reduce clients in the run or top up — you’ll see an error code on screen.

        Getting started First login

        Sign in with your Client ID and access code (from your welcome message). Keep them private.

        1. Open Overview to see quotas (documents, email, WhatsApp, social).
        2. Connect tools under Integrations (mailbox, social, WhatsApp) when your plan allows.
        3. Use Billing & Renewal for upgrades and add-ons — prices stay in one place.
        Documents My Documents · Generate

        My Documents lists files the agent or your team produced. Preview/download when a Drive link is available.

        Generate a Document: pick a type → fill instructions → send. Grey cards mean that type is not in quota or not available. Preview with placeholders does not use a document unit.

        Social Calendar Posts · media
        1. Connect Facebook, Instagram, etc. under Integrations first.
        2. Generate or schedule content in batches (your plan sets how many posts/calendars).
        3. Review each post → Approve → send when ready. Upload images where the form allows.
        Email Activity Replies · resend

        Shows messages the agent drafted or sent from your connected mailbox. Private mode hides content from admin.

        • Draft saved · not sent — connect/reconnect the mailbox, then use Resend once.
        • We never send a second reply on a thread that already sent successfully.
        • Logs are cleaned after about 14 days.
        WhatsApp Activity API · team

        Inbox for API-connected numbers and team handoffs. Connect an API number from this screen or Integrations. Activity is retained about 7 days.

        My Leads Free list · offers
        1. Add lead or Upload CSV (free).
        2. Filter by stage (New → … → Customer / Lost). Use All stages to see everyone.
        3. Optional: follow-up tasks, product catalog, offers. Notify leads uses email/WhatsApp quotas — not free messaging.
        4. HubSpot / Zoho / Salesforce stay under paid Integrations.
        Needs reply One queue

        Combined queue: social comments waiting attention, email/WhatsApp items, and overdue lead follow-ups. Open an item, then jump to the right screen to act.

        Comment replies AI · spam
        1. Buy the comment reply / spam add-on if needed (Billing).
        2. Connect social under Integrations. For TikTok AI comments use Business TikTok.
        3. Here: filter platform/dates, pick a moderated post, turn AI replies on/off, review comments.
        Integrations Connect accounts

        Connect or remove social, mailbox, WhatsApp API, and paid CRM. After connecting, use Social Calendar, Email Activity, or Comment replies to work with the data.
