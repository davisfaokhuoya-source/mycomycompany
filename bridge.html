        Connect Client Email

        Gmail — client authorizes themselves: generates a link you send to the client. They log into their own Gmail and grant access; nothing is typed here.

        Microsoft / Outlook / Office 365 — client authorizes themselves: same idea as Gmail, but for Outlook.com or a company's Microsoft 365 mailbox.

        Custom mail (Zoho Mail, cPanel-hosted, or anything with a REST API): Apps Script can't speak raw IMAP/SMTP to an arbitrary server, so this needs the client's provider (or a small relay in front of it) to expose three REST endpoints — list unread, get one message, and send.

        Manual connect (just register the address, no live connection): useful for record-keeping only — the agent won't be able to read or send from this mailbox until it's connected via one of the options above.
