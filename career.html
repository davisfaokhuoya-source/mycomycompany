    Bridge Partner Training Material

    A reference summary of the full Bridge Partner training — what we sell, how the four levels work, our market, our competitors' weaknesses, objection-handling, and channel-by-channel marketing. Use the Training Academy tab for the level-by-level video training.

    Who We Are Section 2
    The Victorias Ltd is a Nigerian company based in Abuja: one B2B partner from registration to scale. We're CAC-registered ourselves, NDPA-aligned, and NDA-backed — every client signs a Non-Disclosure Agreement before we touch their information, and every client receives a written proposal with defined deliverables and pricing before they pay a single naira. No hidden retainers, no scope creep. We blend AI tools with real human oversight: an AI agent drafts documents and tracks deadlines, but a real person reviews every output before it reaches the client.
    What We Sell Sections 3–5
    CAC & registration: Business Name registration, Company (LLC) incorporation, NGO/Association registration, Annual Filing, Upgrade, Certified True Copies, and Change of Director/Information.

    Compliance & government-facing: SCUML, PENCOM, BPP, ITF, and NSITF — the gatekeepers to government and corporate contracts — plus Trademark registration. Client says "I want to bid for government jobs"? That's a bundle sale.

    Admin, content & growth: CV Refining, AI Power VA, Company Portfolio/Profile, and our monthly Premium Packages — Starter Ops, Growth Ops, Enterprise Ops, Digital Launch, Digital Accelerate, and Digital Scale. Premium packages pay commission every month the client stays active, not just once.
    Your Path: Bridge Partner → Consultant → Senior Consultant → Area Manager Sections 6–9
    Bridge Partner (you, Level 1): sell CAC Business Name, Company, and NGO/Association registration. Find people who aren't properly registered and start the conversation — volume is your friend here.

    Consultant (Level 2): adds Annual Filing, Upgrade, Certified Copies, Change of Director, SCUML, CV Refining, AI Power VA, Company Portfolio/Profile, and Starter Ops. Go back to every client you registered as a Bridge Partner — follow-ups convert easily.

    Senior Consultant (Level 3): unlocks PENCOM, BPP, ITF, NSITF, Trademark, and Growth Ops. Conversations get more consultative — you're positioning a business for bigger money.

    Area Manager (Level 4): every service, including Digital Launch, Accelerate, and Scale. You'll also mentor Bridge Partners and Consultants coming up beneath you.
    Team structure & the Lead role. After onboarding, you will join a 4-person team within a few weeks. Each team has 3 Bridge Partners and 1 Lead. As a Lead, you will manage team productivity and ensure everyone hits their individual and group tasks. This leadership role pays an extra 3% of your team's total generated revenue. To become a Lead, you must be recommended by our AI coach, which continuously tracks your daily activities, performance metrics, and overall progress.
    Know Your Market & Competition Sections 11–12
    Your biggest market: unregistered traders, importers, creators, tailors, caterers, freelancers, and side-hustlers. Also target already-registered SMEs ignoring compliance, unformalized NGOs/churches/community groups, and anyone chasing government or corporate contracts.

    Competitors typically take full payment upfront with no written scope, go quiet for weeks, have no NDA or data-protection process, or quietly add extra charges. Some disappear after payment. Our fix: a written proposal before any payment, a signed NDA on every engagement, one accountable company (not an individual), and one partner across the whole journey.
    Overcoming Objections Section 13
    "I'll do it myself on the CAC portal." Most people give up mid-process on document formatting and rejected uploads — we handle that so they don't lose weeks.

    "It's too expensive." Reframe as protection, not expense — a missing CAC certificate can cost an entire contract or partnership.

    "I already have someone doing it." Ask how long they've had the client's documents; offer a free second opinion if it's been weeks with no update.

    "I don't trust sending documents online." Lead with the NDA and NDPA-alignment.

    "I'll do it later." Create gentle urgency — hold their spot and send requirements now so it's a same-day start whenever they're ready.
    Marketing by Platform Sections 15–21
    Facebook: join active Nigerian business groups, answer real questions, post short native videos and client wins — never blast the same sales post everywhere.

    TikTok: win on volume and authenticity — myth-busting, "day in the life," under 60 seconds, clear call to action ("comment REGISTER").

    Instagram: clean graphics, before/after brand transformations, daily Stories, carousels (problem → solution → how to start). DM anyone who engages within 24 hours.

    X (Twitter): short threads for a formal, business-minded audience; reply genuinely to real business-struggle threads.

    WhatsApp DM: highest-converting channel — personalize the opener, never mass-broadcast, ask one question at a time.

    WhatsApp Groups: become a known helpful presence before posting anything promotional; frame offers as information, not ads.

    WhatsApp Status: post daily — myth-busting, client wins, behind-the-scenes, and a friendly call to action.
    Routine & Professionalism Sections 22–23
    Consistency beats intensity: one status update, one comment/reply, and one direct outreach message every single day. Track every conversation (name, platform, service, date, next follow-up) — most sales are lost from missed follow-up, not a "no."

    Never promise a specific approval timeline or a guaranteed contract win. Never accept payment directly into a personal account. Always lead with the NDA and written-proposal-before-payment policy — it's your strongest trust signal. Keep commission figures general ("up to a percentage commission") with prospects; official onboarding conversations handle the specific numbers.

    Platform Guide & Policies
    How You Became (or Become) a Bridge Partner
    1. Register with a valid signup code — registration is invite-only, never open to the public.
    2. Verify your email with the code sent to you.
    3. Watch the training module for your current level in the Training Academy tab.
    4. Take the assessment. Pass, and you're approved immediately. If you don't pass, you can retake once after a 7-day wait — a second miss closes the application.
    5. Once approved, you can submit clients and start earning commission.
    Commission Structure
    Bridge Partner Up to 7%
    Consultant Up to 10%
    Senior Consultant Up to 15%
    Area Manager Up to 20%

    One-time vs. recurring earnings:

    • One-time services (e.g. CAC/company registration) pay commission once, when the job is completed and paid.
    • Subscription/plan services (e.g. a client on Starter Ops) pay commission on every renewal, for as long as that client's subscription stays active.
    Using the Platform
    • My Available Tasks / Submit Client — the services you're allowed to offer at your level, and where you submit new client jobs.
    • My Records — every job you've submitted and its status/earnings.
    • Earnings & Payout — your balance and how to request payment.
    • Upgrade Assessment — check eligibility and test for your next tier.
    • AI Coach — your weekly target and progress, custom tips and ready-to-post social content for TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp, and a chat assistant for questions.
    • Messages — direct messages with the admin team.
    • Complaint Tickets — raise an issue for admin to review.

    Policies

    These are the clauses of our master Company Policy that specifically concern you as a Partner (Career Hub Marketer/Consultant/Senior Consultant/Area Manager) — drawn from the Agent & Partner Policy, Confidentiality Policy, and Anti-Fraud Policy sections. This is a summary for quick reference; the full legal document remains the authoritative version and governs in the event of any difference.

    Who you are — Independent Contractor Agent & Partner Policy
    You operate as an independent contractor, not an employee, of The Victorias Ltd. You're free to set your own hours and marketing methods within these policies, you're not entitled to employee benefits (pension, health insurance, paid leave), and you're responsible for your own taxes and business costs. In every client-facing conversation you must identify yourself as a Partner or Authorised Marketer — never as an employee.
    Marketing standards & client communication Agent & Partner Policy
    Only represent prices, timelines, and services that are current and confirmed through official channels — never invent or guarantee outcomes we haven't promised. No deceptive or high-pressure sales tactics. Respond to client enquiries within 24 hours. Client personal data collected while marketing may only be submitted through the official portal — never stored, sold, or shared elsewhere. You may use approved brand materials from the Media Resources section, but may not create or publish your own unapproved materials representing The Victorias Ltd.
    Payments — official channels only Agent & Partner Policy
    Under no circumstances may you collect payment from a client directly. Every payment must flow through the official Flutterwave link. Partners found collecting client payments outside this system are terminated immediately and may face criminal liability for fraud.
    Tier levels & commission Agent & Partner Policy
    Partner (Level 1) Up to 7% commission
    Consultant (Level 2) Up to 10% commission
    Senior Consultant (Level 3) Up to 15% commission
    Area Manager (Level 4) Up to 20% commission
    Each level unlocks a wider range of services you're allowed to offer. Commission rates and tier thresholds may be adjusted with 14 days' advance notice.
    Conduct that ends your account Agent & Partner Policy
    Submitting fake or duplicate client records, collecting payment outside official channels, using our brand to solicit clients for a competing or personal business, recruiting other Partners away from the platform, sharing confidential platform information, or impersonating an employee — all lead to immediate account termination and possible legal action.
    Confidentiality Confidentiality Policy
    Commission structures, tier criteria, internal platform processes, assessment questions/answers, and any client data you come across are confidential. Use this information only for its intended purpose, never disclose it to a third party, and return or destroy it if your engagement ends. This obligation continues even after you leave the platform.
    Anti-fraud & reporting Anti-Fraud Policy
    We have zero tolerance for fraud, bribery, and corruption. If you suspect wrongdoing — yours, another Partner's, or a client's — you can report it through Complaint Tickets or to management@thevictorias.com.ng. Good-faith reports are never retaliated against.
    Weekly reporting & targets Platform Policy
    You'll be asked for a short weekly check-in — highlights, challenges, and your plan for the week ahead. Our AI Coach sets an individual weekly target from your recent performance. A first missed target gets a supportive check-in; a second consecutive miss flags your account for admin review; a third consecutive miss results in a one-tier adjustment (reversible as your performance improves) — unless you're already a Partner (Level 1), in which case it goes to admin for a decision.
